Methodology & Engine Reference

Per-shift segmentation

Time segments are clustered into shifts before scoring. A new shift begins when the previous segment ended with an "Out" punch (shift-end) OR there is a gap > 2 hours between an "Out" timestamp and the next "In." This correctly identifies night-shift workers who appear twice on a single calendar date — the tail of one shift in early AM and the head of the next late PM — and prevents false-positive "missed punch" flags.

Two tiers of technical OT

  • Confirmed Inflation — mathematically certain over-pay from punch artifacts: worked > elapsed within a shift, or meal > 60 min. Counted at full value.
  • Potential Inflation — pattern flags where the underlying reality is ambiguous. No meal punch on shifts > 6 hrs could be a skipped break (real liability, paid correctly) OR a missed punch (technical OT). Recovered at the Missed-Punch Recovery % in the controls.

Real OT vs. Technical OT (multi-day mode)

When the loaded dataset spans multiple days, the engine computes weekly hours per worker and applies the standard FLSA 40-hour threshold. Hours above 40 are split between Technical OT (punch-artifact inflation, capped at OT total) and Real OT (the residual — actual extra labor delivered).

Severity logic

  • Critical — confirmed inflation > 1 hour
  • Review — two or more non-info flags
  • Flag — one non-info flag
  • Clean — no anomalies detected
  • Informational — night-shift turnover or shift carrying to next day (not a real flag)

Local data storage

Uploaded data is parsed entirely in your browser and persisted in localStorage under the key highland_tech_ot:v1. Nothing leaves your machine. Settings (rate, OT multiplier, recovery %, meal mode) persist across sessions. Use Clear to remove all stored data.

Expected file format

Workday "Time Block Data" export, Excel (.xlsx) or CSV. The parser is forgiving about column order and accepts common naming variations. Required: a row per time segment with Worker, Reported Date, In Time, Out Time, Out Type ("Out" or "Meal"), and Reported Quantity (hours).
